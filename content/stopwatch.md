weekend comes closer each day  
cold steps disturbing the tranquil silence  
tick-tock the clock moves  
and the sun starts going back down  
this evening the tick-tock slows to a halt  
has the battery run out? you wonder  
but then out of the corner of your eye  
you see her, is she whispering something?  
you cant bring yourself to turn towards her  
she starts approaching, you break a cold sweat  
you try to run, scream, hide but you find your body stuck, unable to move  
she is approaching, whisper getting louder  
she is breathing down your neck now   
and finally you hear her clearly  
"i want to ????? ?????"  
what? what do you want?  
she is gone, clock ticking again  
you wonder if it was a hallucination
