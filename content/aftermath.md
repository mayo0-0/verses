when your brain drowns in desire  
when you leave the controls of your self  
when desire drives & you sit in the back  
you learn that you have no choice  
when desire and restraint battle  
when you finally choose restraint  
and the battle ends  
smoke rising from the fields  
as it clears up, restraint actually wins  
a smile creeps up on your face  
but you must hold your breath still  
as there are many more battles to come  
and restraint is still weaker than desire
