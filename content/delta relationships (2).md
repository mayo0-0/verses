many upon a times there was this boy
he would make a friend, hit it off good
they would know what the other was upto
all the time they would know
like a rocket as it launches
their intensity would explode take off
but as time passes velocity would fade
heat slowly shuts off velocity decreases
now they would barely talk
the boy is sensitive so he would notice
his rocket speed ship slowing down
the rocket becomes a plane then car,.cycle
and then the ship would be on life support
unable to continue only sending signals 
to keep it in a persistent vegetative state
the boy sometimes thought
maybe this ship was destined to sink
but every ship that would launch, sink
there must be something wrong with the boy then surely
