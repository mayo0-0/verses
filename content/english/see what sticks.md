taming a skewed mind-  
is not an easy task  
habits that try to survive  
keep poking the brain ruthlessly  
but as the god of my mindscape  
i decide which habits shall survive  
and which shall go extinct  
an illusion has been created though  
by the opposite forces  
made the king think he is a citizen  
but when the illusion breaks  
and the king realises he is actually god  
then the great apocalypse shall occur  
and only the habits that make it  
onto the Noah's Ark will survive
