filed with frustration i go to the well  
its a barren empty well creepy and quiet  
whats down there nobody could tell  
i screamed in it with all my might  
the evidence of my internal fight  
the well swallowed the scream  
didnt even spare the echo  
i didnt know the well was evil  
as i went back and looked in the mirror  
i saw the well behind me  
and since then the well has stayed  
it keeps echoing back the past  
i wonder how long i can last
