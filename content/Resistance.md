Old habits whisper in my ear  
They tell me to give into fear  
I am a slave who has had a taste-  
Of an actual anchorless freedom  
Unable to go back to my old masters  
I must find a new way of living  

Although liberating, freedom is a cruel joke  
In truth it's much more punishing than those old masters  
Maybe I should go back to them?  
Beg for forgiveness and ask to slave away again?  
Funny thoughts like that poisoning my mind  
As freedom cracks it's whips too-  
Softer but still a whip.  
Was there something else I could have done?
