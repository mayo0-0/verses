started yelling at the top of my lungs  
kept punching and punching myself  
that has gone on for how long now?  
my throat has lost its voice  
my scream, dissolved into silence  
my hands stopped feeling the punch  
the me getting punched also looks dead  
as i come back to my senses i realise  
i killed him long ago  
i have been punching a dead body  
all this time
