When I cried that night, nature cried with me
i could feel the wails of trees
the angst of the wind
the absence of stillness
the love of the moon hidden
by the doubts of the coulds

When such a storm strikes
you do not stop it
you cannot stop it 
you can only let it pass
and be thankful that the violence
spared your lives and your loved ones

Even if you close all doors and windows
Try to hide in your house, in your blanket
You still cannot escape the consequences of your actions
